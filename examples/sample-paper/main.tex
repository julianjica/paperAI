\begin{abstract}
We study a firm whose manager has limited attention and must decide which decisions to delegate. Delegation economizes on attention but exposes the firm to misaligned subordinates. We show that the optimal scope of delegation is non-monotone in the manager's attention capacity, and we characterize when the firm should integrate or outsource a task.
\end{abstract}
